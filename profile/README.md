## Contributing & Community

We develop entirely in the open. Our work sits at the intersection of core computer science, systems engineering, and programming language theory. If you are a systems engineer, compiler researcher, or protocol developer interested in redefining stateful computing from first principles:

* Explore the [repositories](https://github.com/ConvergentMachines/repositories).
* Open [issues](https://github.com/ConvergentMachines/issues) or join [discussions](https://github.com/ConvergentMachines/discussions) on our active engineering efforts.
* Reach out if you want to collaborate on the theoretical paper series or runtime implementations.
